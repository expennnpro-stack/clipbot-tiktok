Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

ClipBot is a personal desktop application operated by Expennn. It has no server and no database of users.

What data is used

When the owner connects their TikTok account, ClipBot receives an access token and basic profile information (display name) from TikTok. This is used only to send the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account.

Where data is stored

Tokens and videos are stored only on the owner's computer. They are never sent to the developer or to any third party, and they are not sold or shared.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect TikTok at any time from ClipBot or from TikTok's account settings, and delete the local files.
